Subject: Customer requesting refund after 45 days

Customer purchased a subscription 45 days ago and is requesting a full
refund, stating they never used the product.

Resolution: Our standard refund policy allows full refunds within 30 days
of purchase, no questions asked. Requests made between 31-60 days are
eligible for a 50% partial refund at the agent's discretion, provided the
account shows minimal usage. Requests after 60 days are not eligible for
any refund except in cases of billing error or fraud. For this case
(45 days, no usage), offer the 50% partial refund and document the
reason in the ticket.
