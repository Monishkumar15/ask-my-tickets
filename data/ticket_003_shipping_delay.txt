Subject: Order delayed beyond estimated delivery date

Customer's order was estimated to arrive in 5 business days but it has
now been 9 business days with no delivery.

Resolution: For domestic orders delayed more than 7 business days past
the estimated delivery date, agents should first check the carrier
tracking link for updates. If tracking shows no movement in the last 3
days, file a "lost in transit" claim with the carrier and offer the
customer either a full reshipment at no cost or a full refund, whichever
they prefer. Delays under 7 business days are considered normal and do
not qualify for compensation.
