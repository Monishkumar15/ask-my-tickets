Subject: Refund request for a defective item

Customer says the product arrived broken and wants a refund.

Resolution: Defective items are eligible for a full refund regardless of
purchase date, as long as the customer reports the defect within 90 days
of delivery and provides a photo of the damage. This overrides the
standard 30/31-60/60+ day refund window, which only applies to
non-defective returns.
