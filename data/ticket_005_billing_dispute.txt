Subject: Customer disputes a duplicate charge

Customer noticed two identical charges of $29.99 on the same day and
believes they were billed twice by mistake.

Resolution: Duplicate charges on the same day for the same amount are
usually caused by a payment retry after a temporarily declined card, and
are almost always automatically reversed by the payment processor within
5-7 business days. Confirm with the customer that only one charge appears
as "settled" and the other shows as "pending" or "reversed" in their bank
statement. If both charges show as fully settled after 7 business days,
escalate to the billing team for a manual refund of the duplicate amount.
