Subject: How to cancel a subscription and data retention

Customer wants to cancel their subscription and asks what happens to
their saved data.

Resolution: Subscriptions can be cancelled anytime from Account Settings
> Subscription > Cancel Plan, effective at the end of the current billing
cycle (no partial refunds for the remaining days). After cancellation,
customer data is retained for 90 days in case the customer wants to
reactivate. After 90 days, all data is permanently deleted and cannot be
recovered. Inform the customer of this 90-day window when processing a
cancellation request.
