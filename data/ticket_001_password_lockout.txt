Subject: Password reset not working after failed logins

Customer reports that after 3 failed login attempts, their account gets
locked and the "Forgot Password" email never arrives.

Resolution: This is expected behavior. After 3 failed login attempts, the
account locks for 15 minutes for security reasons. During the lockout
window, no reset email is sent by design. Ask the customer to wait 15
minutes and then use the "Forgot Password" link again — it will work
normally once the lockout period ends. If the customer still cannot log
in after 15 minutes, escalate to Tier 2 for a manual unlock.
